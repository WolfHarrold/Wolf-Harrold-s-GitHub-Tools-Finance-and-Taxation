Refund questions

When is my tax return due?

Lodge your 2025–26 return by 31 October 2026 if you do it yourself. A registered tax agent can lodge later if you are on their client list before 31 October.

Why is the estimate different from myTax?

myTax uses the exact figures from your employer, bank and health fund. It also applies offsets and rules this tool does not include. Use the myTax figure as your final result.

What is the Medicare levy surcharge?

An extra 1% to 1.5% for people without private hospital cover whose income is over the threshold. It is separate from the 2% Medicare levy.

How does a study loan change my refund?

The ATO adds your compulsory study loan repayment to your tax bill. It is not a penalty. It reduces your loan balance.

What records do I need?

Keep records for every claim. The working from home fixed rate needs a record of the hours you worked from home. The cents per kilometre method needs a way to show how you worked out your work kilometres, such as a diary.

Pay questions

Why is my payslip different?

Employers use ATO withholding tables, which round each pay. Salary sacrifice, extra super and other deductions also change the amount. Use Payslip check in the Tools tab to compare one payslip.

Should I claim the tax-free threshold?

Claim it from one employer only, usually the one that pays you the most. If you claim it from two employers, too little tax is withheld and you can get a tax bill.

Is super included in my pay?

Usually not. Your employer pays the 12% super guarantee on top of your pay, into your fund, each payday. Tick My amount includes super if your salary package includes it.

Is overtime taxed more?

No. Overtime is taxed at the same rates as other income. A large pay can have more tax withheld because the tables treat it like a higher yearly income. You get back any extra when you lodge.

Check your pay, step by step

  1. Find your award. Use the list above or the Fair Work Pay Calculator.
  2. Find your classification level. Read the level descriptions in the award. Match them to the work you do.
  3. Compare your base rate. Compare the award rate with the rate on your payslip.
  4. Check extra pay. Check penalty rates, overtime and allowances for each shift.
  5. Check your payslip. Your employer must give you a payslip within 1 working day of payday.
  6. Act on underpayment. Speak to your employer first. Keep notes. Then phone the Fair Work Ombudsman on 13 13 94.

Payday Super started 1 July 2026

Your employer must now pay your super each payday, not each quarter. Your fund must receive it within 7 business days after payday.

  1. Open your super fund app or ATO online services.
  2. Compare each deposit with the super amount on your payslip.
  3. If a payment is missing, ask your employer. Then tell the ATO.
NOTESuper guarantee stays at 12%. From 1 July 2026 it is worked out on "qualifying earnings".
